# Create Solana Liquidity Pool

Easily create a liquidity pool for your Solana token. The fastest way to deploy Solana tokens and launch your project with SPLForge.

## How To Create a Liquidity Pool

1. Select the token that you just created.
2. Enter the amount tokens you would like to include in your liquidity pool. (Recommended 95% or more)
3. Select a base token, SOL is recommended.
4. Enter the amount of SOL you would like to pair with your token. (Recommended 10+ SOL)
5. Click 'Initialize Liquidity Pool' and approve transaction. The cost to create a liquidity pool is 1 SOL + SPL Fees (aprox 1 SOL).

In return, you will receive Liquidity pool tokens. To burn liquidity so it shows locked, head to sol-incinerator.com.

*Note: The amount of SOL you enter as your starting LP determines the starting price of your token.*
*USDC Token doesn't work on Devnet.*
